01

Field note 01 · Engineering

Building again

Back to spending more time thinking about systems, product decisions, and how AI can become genuinely useful rather than just impressive in a demo.

Around eight years of full-stack, backend and cloud work so far, more of it lately spent wiring AI services into real products. Before that came an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Northumbria University London.

useful > impressive

02

Field note 02 · Barbering

Behind the chair

Barbering taught me things about people, trust, and running a business that I wouldn't have learned by writing software alone. Those lessons still influence the products I want to build.

It also turned into software: I built a tool to help a barbershop manage appointments, payments, expenses and the rest of the day-to-day running of the business.

→ and then it became software

04 Afterword

Sussing it out.

I'm interested in the space between technology and the real world — the problems people deal with every day, the tools that make those problems easier, and the creative process of turning an idea into something useful.

Right now, that means learning more about AI, building products, finding my place in engineering again, and holding on to the things that make life interesting outside a screen.

Moving towards greater things.

— Ayush.
